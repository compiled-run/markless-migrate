Remix Notes: a migration from Remix to Markless

The Remix Indie Stack notes app (remix-run/indie-stack, now archived: email and password sign-up and login with a cookie session, SQLite through Prisma, notes you create and delete), ported to Markless and proven with @markless/migrate: 26 of 26 route checks green, signed in and signed out, with every accepted difference listed in the report.

  • The front door: every checked route is switched to the Markless app; anything else still goes to the old app
  • The old app (Remix 2, unchanged source)
  • The new app (Markless 0.4.0)
  • The check report: every route, old against new, as anonymous and signed in

Demo login: rachel@remix.run / racheliscool (the stack's seed user), or sign up with any email and a password of 8 characters or more. A session from one app works in the other.

No hosted database was available to this demo, so each app copies the seeded SQLite file to its serverless function's /tmp when an instance starts. Sign-ups and notes stay only while that instance lives, and two instances do not see each other's changes.
